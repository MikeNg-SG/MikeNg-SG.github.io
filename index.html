Financial RAG System

September 2026

A question-answering system over SEC 10-K annual reports for Apple, Microsoft, Amazon and Google. You ask a question about a company's business or risks, and it answers with citations back to the filing.

  • Pipeline: download filings, extract and clean text, split by report section (business, risk factors, MD&A, financials), chunk, embed, store in Weaviate
  • Hybrid search (BM25 + dense vectors), then a cross-encoder reranks results before Claude generates the answer
  • Currently building an evaluation set of test questions with known answers to measure retrieval and answer quality

Stack: Python, Weaviate, sentence-transformers, Claude API, OpenAI API uv

Privacy-Aware Multi-LLM Routing (Capstone)

November 2025

Won the DroneShield Industry Award at the UTS AI Showcase 2025. The system routes user prompts to different LLMs depending on whether they contain sensitive personal information.

Transfer Learning Benchmark: ResNet50 vs EfficientNetB0

May 2025

Compared two ImageNet-pretrained CNNs on a 5-class footwear dataset (Ballet Flat, Boat, Brogue, Clog, Sneaker) of about 13,700 images from Kaggle. Both models were fully fine-tuned with the same classification head, augmentation, optimizer and training budget.

  • ResNet50 reached 85.19% test accuracy, compared with 73.83% for EfficientNetB0
  • Per-class analysis showed the gap came mostly from two visually similar classes, Ballet Flat and Boat, where ResNet50's recall was about 30 points higher
  • EfficientNetB0 trained faster (~32 vs ~58 minutes) with about 6 times fewer parameters, but its validation performance was much less stable

Stack: Python, TensorFlow/Keras, Google Colab
